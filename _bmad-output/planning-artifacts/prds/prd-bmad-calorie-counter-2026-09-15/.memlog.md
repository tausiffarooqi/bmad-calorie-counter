- (decision) Every meal/snack entry (photo or text) gets timestamped on submission -- not captured in the original intent doc

- (event) Research grounding: photo-based calorie estimation typically runs ~11-20% MAPE even for leading apps, systematically underestimates mixed/complex meals; industry commonly caveats accuracy as directional not clinical-grade, uses retry/manual-correction flows (matches our insufficient-detail retry rule); logging speed and adaptive time-of-day recommendations are rare differentiators (whitespace); retention is brutal industry-wide without a habit-forming loop
- (decision) Success metric: user can log a meal and receive a recommendation in under 5 seconds
- (decision) Photo retention: discard the original photo after analysis; retain the AI-inferred text description as part of the permanent log record
